Derscription:

Samosa is more than just a snack—it’s a golden triangle of comfort and spice. These crisp, flaky pastries are filled with a savory mixture of spiced potatoes, peas, and sometimes meat, then deep-fried to irresistible perfection. Whether served hot at roadside stalls or shared during festive gatherings, samosa carries with it the warmth of tradition and the joy of togetherness. Its crunchy shell and flavorful filling make it a timeless favorite, often outshining more elaborate dishes. One bite, and you’re transported to a world where indulgence is wrapped in layers of spice and crunch.
